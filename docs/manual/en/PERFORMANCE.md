# TRAPSTATION - PERFORMANCE

Release 1.0.0.0 (15 November 2026).

| Control | Function |
|---|---|
| BLACK 1-6 | Mute tracks directly |
| OCT+ + BLACK | Choose effect target 1-6 |
| BLACK 7 | Select MASTER target |
| BLACK 8-11 | Bar 1-4; tap again = ALL |

White 1-8: hold effects; 9-16: latch them. K1 FILTER / K2 TUNE / K3 FDBK / K4 DLY.

1 KILL · 2 ECHO · 3 VERB · 4 COMB

5 SWEEP · 6 WASH · 7 RISE · 8 STUT

All six tracks stay visible. Leaving the page ends its active effects.

K2 TUNE and K3 FDBK are the COMB controls. K4 DLY changes the selected channel send, or all track sends together on MASTER. During ECHO, this send remains the return value. Effect latches are not saved.

## Location and routing

Open PERFORMANCE from HOME, after SCENES (hold HOME and turn SELECT). It is available from a track or the FX track. GLO PERF is the separate macro/crossfader/beat-repeat page; SCENES stores mute combinations.

An effect acts on the channel selected when it was enabled. You can select the next target while an existing effect continues on its original channel. Releasing a held effect or unlatching it restores its temporary changes. Leaving PERFORMANCE ends all its effects, including latches. These states are not saved.

| Key | Effect | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | KILL | Mute the target; MASTER mutes all tracks while effect tails can continue. |
| 2 | ECHO | Full delay send for an echo throw; echoes continue after release. |
| 3 | VERB | Full reverb send for a reverb throw. |
| 4 | COMB | Full Comb on the target; K2/K3 shape it. DAMP stays on COMB. |
| 5 | SWEEP | Comb TUNE rises two octaves over one bar. |
| 6 | WASH | Low-pass closes over one bar. |
| 7 | RISE | High-pass opens over one bar. |
| 8 | STUT | A 1/16 gate, clocked during playback. |

## Display

All six tracks stay visible in two columns, with MASTER across the bottom. The chosen target has a red frame. Muted channels are grey and marked M. A white line under a channel identifies an effect acting on it. Active effect tiles are red; latched tiles have a white outline. SWEEP, WASH and RISE show progress. The four value cards are FILTER, TUNE, FDBK and DLY. White-key labels distinguish 1-8 HOLD from 9-16 LATCH.
